TEKVEX / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the TekVex Labs website and related services.

01 / INFORMATION

Information you provide

Depending on how you interact with TekVex Labs, you may provide information such as your name, email address, purchase information, or information included in a support request.

02 / TRANSACTIONS

Payment information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. TekVex Labs does not need to directly collect or store complete payment-card information when the transaction is handled by the designated payment provider.

03 / AUTOMATIC DATA

Technical information

Standard technical information may be processed by hosting, analytics, security, or other service providers. This can include information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, and access logs.

04 / PURPOSE

How information is used

Information may be used to provide products, process transactions, respond to support requests, maintain website security, improve services, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

05 / PROVIDERS

Service providers

TekVex Labs may rely on third-party providers for hosting, payments, analytics, communications, security, and digital delivery. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

06 / SECURITY

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through our services. However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07 / RETENTION

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate interests.

08 / THIRD-PARTY LINKS

External services

The TekVex Labs website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we recommend reviewing them before providing information.

09 / CHANGES

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

10 / CONTACT

Privacy questions

If you have a question regarding privacy or the handling of your information, contact TekVex Labs.

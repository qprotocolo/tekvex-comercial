TEKVEX / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about TekVex Labs digital products, purchasing, delivery, and support.

01 / PRODUCTS

About the products

What do I receive after purchasing?

Products available through TekVex Labs are digital resources. The exact contents and delivery method are described on each individual product page and during checkout.

Are the products physical?

No. TekVex Labs products are digital products. No physical item is shipped to the customer.

Are the products suitable for beginners?

It depends on the individual product. Each product page should indicate its intended audience, scope, and technical level.

02 / PURCHASE

Purchasing and delivery

How do I purchase a product?

Select the product you want, review its information, and use the purchase button to continue to the checkout provided for that product.

What payment methods are available?

Available payment methods are displayed directly by the checkout provider at the time of purchase.

When will I receive my product?

Digital delivery is handled according to the delivery process specified by the checkout provider and the individual product.

I completed payment but did not receive the product. What should I do?

First, check the email address used during checkout and your spam or promotions folders. If the issue remains, contact TekVex Labs with your purchase information so we can investigate.
